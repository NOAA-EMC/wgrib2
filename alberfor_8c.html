Detailed Description

Albers Conical Equal Area - Forward Transformation.

PURPOSE: Transforms input longitude and latitude to Easting and Northing for the Albers Conical Equal Area projection. The longitude and latitude must be in radians. The Easting and Northing values will be returned in meters.
