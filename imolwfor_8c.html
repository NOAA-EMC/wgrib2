Detailed Description

Interrupted Mollweide - Forward Transformation.

PURPOSE: Transforms input longitude and latitude to Easting and Northing for the Interrupted Mollweide projection. The longitude and latitude must be in radians. The Easting and Northing values will be returned in meters.
