Detailed Description

Lambert Conformal Conic - Inverse Transformation.

PURPOSE: Transforms input Easting and Northing to longitude and latitude for the Lambert Conformal Conic projection. The Easting and Northing must be in meters. The longitude and latitude values will be returned in radians.
