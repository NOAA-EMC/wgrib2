Detailed Description

Stereographic - Forward Transformation.

PURPOSE: Transforms input longitude and latitude to Easting and Northing for the Stereographic projection. The longitude and latitude must be in radians. The Easting and Northing values will be returned in meters.

T. Mittan

March, 1993
